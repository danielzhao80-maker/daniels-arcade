🐾 Summon Battlers

Summon your pets and battle! Collect pets from eggs, build your deck of 6, and smash the enemy base.
🪙 0🗺️ Stage 1/30⭐ 0🐾 0/1024
How to play: tap a pet card to summon it (it costs ⚡ energy, which fills up over time). Your pets walk right and fight. Both bases shoot enemies that get close. Break the enemy base 🏯 before they break yours 🏰!

🗺️ Campaign — 5 worlds, 30 stages

Every 6th stage is a 👹 boss. Win with more base left for more ⭐.

🐾 My Pets & Deck

Tap a pet to see it: put it in your deck, or level it up with shards (from duplicate eggs) and coins. 🔓 Abilities unlock at Lv 3, Lv 6 and an ULTIMATE at Lv 10!

🥚 Hatch Eggs

Hatch an egg to get a pet. Got it already? You get shards to level it up instead.
